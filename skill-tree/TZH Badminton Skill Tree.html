羽毛球的成长是一段长期的旅程，而非一次性的考试。技能树帮助球员、家长与教练清楚地看见球员在这段旅程中的位置 —— 已经走过的、正在攀登的，以及前方的路。 Badminton development is a long-term journey, not a single test. The Skill Tree helps players, parents and coaches clearly see where a player stands along that journey — what has been travelled, what is being climbed, and what lies ahead.

02 同一把量尺 · One Shared Standard
The Mastery Scale
五星精通量表
接触 → 精熟
Introduced → Mastered
技能树里的每一项技术，都用这五个等级来评估。它把「学会了没有」的模糊问题，变成一条清晰可见的进步阶梯。 Every skill in the Skill Tree is rated on these five levels. It turns the vague question of "can they do it?" into a clear, visible ladder of progress.
Introduced 已接触
已开始学习该技术，需要大量指导。The player has been introduced to the skill but still requires significant guidance.
Developing 初步掌握
已理解动作，但稳定性仍需提升。The player understands the movement, but consistency is still developing.
Competent 稳定执行
训练中能够稳定完成动作。The player can perform the skill consistently during training.
Game Ready 实战应用
能够在比赛中自信地运用。The player is able to apply the skill confidently during real match situations.
Mastered 精熟运用
能够灵活应用，并根据不同比赛情况做出调整。The player demonstrates excellent consistency, adaptability and confidence under different situations.
评估时，教练在每项技术旁的五颗星中填涂对应的星级。空白的星星，正是尚未走完的路 —— 它们让下一步的方向一目了然。 During an assessment, the coach fills the stars beside each skill to the level reached. The empty stars are simply the road not yet travelled — making the next step easy to see.
