Admin Help
Session
Set tonight’s date and tap players to add them to the line-up. In the List view, the Level picker (1.0–7.0) and the Girl & Mixed toggles set a player’s rating and who plays mixed doubles, and these drive level-balanced match generation. Pts is this month’s points (+2 a session, editable by hand) and goes back to zero for everyone when the month ends, whether or not they made the Monthly draw. Life next to it is their lifetime total: every point they have ever earned, never reset. Members never see it; it is on the Players list, the Accounts rows and the Payments member popup, for you only.
Courts
Build rounds, choose which courts are active, and manage who’s resting. Each court’s current match shows live on the viewer screen. When play ends, press End of the day (bottom of the tab) to create tonight’s payment records.
Payments
The fee depends on how long each person stayed: 2h · RM20 or 3h · RM25. The Session tab sets the default for the night; after End of the day every player in the line-up gets a row here at that default. Tap 2h or 3h on a row for early leavers, tap Cash, TnG or DuitNow as each person pays (one tap), or Unpaid to undo. Paid rows drop to the bottom so whoever still owes stays at the top. Tap the amount for a one-off custom fee. Pressing End of the day again adds players who joined later and drops anyone you unticked in the meantime (unless they already paid), so the ticked line-up is always what counts. Switch to By member to see everyone’s running balance across every night on record; tap a member for their points, total outstanding and a night-by-night breakdown, and settle any night right there. A night is never removed from history while someone on it still owes.
Lucky Draw: Session draws
Every Monday, Friday and Sunday session gets one automatic draw at 9:00 AM three mornings later (Mon → Thu, Fri → Mon, Sun → Wed). A player is in it when they attended and their payment was marked paid before that time, so End of the day and the Payments tab decide who is eligible. How many winners are picked, and what they get, sit with the Prizes list. A draw is never re-run on its own: if the scheduled draw did not happen, Run draw now applies the same rules. If one came out on the wrong line-up — nobody marked paid yet, say — fix the payments, then Re-draw on that night’s card picks new winners on the spot; Remove result clears the winners and leaves the night undrawn instead. The old winners stay in the audit log either way. Players see the winners and the replay video on the public Lucky Draw page; who attended and who paid stays admin-only.
Lucky Draw: Monthly draws
A points draw once a month. Everyone who reaches the points needed (default 80) during the month is in it; points go back to zero for everyone when the month ends: winners, non-winners and anyone who never reached the threshold alike. Only the admin-only lifetime total on the Session tab keeps the full record. With the automatic switch on, the draw runs itself at 9:00 AM on the 1st of the next month; switch it off to run it yourself. Pull players lists everyone at the threshold right now (untick anyone who should sit out), then Run draw now draws this month on the spot. Set how many winners are picked and the prize list, where each prize picks the place it goes to (two prizes on the same place means that winner takes both). Each prize has a quantity (so one winner can take, say, 2 tubes of shuttlecocks), and an optional description; these show on the public Lucky Draw page, and the winner’s prize (with quantity) in the replay video. One draw per month; results are permanent.
Sign-ups
People who asked to join through the public form, newest first. Mark each one contacted once you’ve reached out.
Accounts
Members sign in with a login code like Thomas#123 and see their points, what they owe and their draw wins. Press Assign codes to everyone once, then tap a code to copy it and send it to that member. Tap a member’s name (or Manage) to open their profile: their points, what they owe, their level and their login code are all there and all editable, and Copy invite puts the whole sign-in message on your clipboard. Phone + password sign-in still works for anyone who has one: approve requests, or open Manage → Reveal / temporary password to help with a forgotten password. Deleting an account keeps the player. It only removes the login.
Settings
Share the viewer link, set the daily site access code, change the admin password, pick the phone shortcuts (which tabs sit in the bottom bar on phones, up to 4, with the rest under More), choose which social game days appear on the public sign-up, and set weekly regulars: players who always come on a given day, added to the line-up automatically when the session date lands on that day.
