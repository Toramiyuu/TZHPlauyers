TZH Resident Stringer · 驻店穿线师
Thomas isn't just pulling strings — he tunes your racket to you. He reads your game and recommends the right string and tension so your racket feels exactly how you want it. Thomas 不只是穿线，更为你「调校」球拍。他了解你的打法，为你推荐最合适的线材与磅数，让球拍手感正中你心。
How Thomas recommends · Thomas 如何为你推荐

Your racket 球拍

Frame stiffness & balance set a safe, ideal tension range. 拍框软硬与平衡，决定理想且安全的磅数范围。

Your style & level 打法与水平

Power, control or all-round — each wants a different string. 进攻、控制或全面型，适合不同线材。

Your tension 磅数偏好

The repulsion-vs-control trade-off, dialled to your preferred lbs. 弹力与控球的取舍，依你偏好的磅数调校。

String condition 线况

Tension loss & wear tell us exactly when to restring. 掉磅与磨损，判断最佳换线时机。
